NEON RUSH

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Developer: Zentrix Arcade · App: Neon Rush for Android (com.zentrixarcade.neonrush)

Summary. Neon Rush has no accounts, no logins and no in-app purchases, and Zentrix Arcade does not run any servers that receive your data. The game does include Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to offer optional rewarded video ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it. These Google services collect and share data as described below. This happens even if you never watch an ad, because the ads SDK starts and loads an ad in the background once the game is allowed to request ads.

1. Who is responsible

Neon Rush is developed by Zentrix Arcade. For any privacy question or request, contact us at huydo1805@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

The game saves the following on your device so it can work offline. It is never sent to Zentrix Arcade:

  • game progress: scores, best runs, level and experience, coins, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, achievements, daily and weekly goal progress, and the daily gift status;
  • a count of rewarded ads watched today, used for the daily limit of 10;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, accessibility and display options);
  • an optional "runner name" you can type in your profile.

Google's consent tool also stores your consent choices on the device (for example an IAB TCF consent string). Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this data.

3. Data collected and shared by third-party services

Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google LLC)

Used to load and show optional rewarded video ads. According to Google's disclosures for this SDK, it may automatically collect the following data from your device and share it with Google:

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent choices and where you live. Ads in Neon Rush are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rating "G"). How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Google User Messaging Platform (Google LLC)

When the game starts, it checks with Google whether a consent message is required for your region (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland). If one is required, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested. This check sends technical information such as your IP address and device and app information to Google, and your choices are stored on the device.

Google Play services

The game uses Google Play components that come with Android apps distributed on Google Play (for example to deliver game asset files and to provide the App Set ID used by the ads SDK). Their data handling is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

What Zentrix Arcade receives

We do not receive your advertising ID, location or ad interaction data. Like all AdMob publishers, we can see aggregated reports in our AdMob account (for example how many ads were shown, by country), which do not identify you. We do not sell your personal information.

4. When data is collected

  • At launch: the consent check described in section 3 runs.
  • If ads may be requested (no consent needed in your region, or after you have made your choice): the Google Mobile Ads SDK starts and loads a rewarded ad in the background so it is ready if you tap "Watch Ad". It does this even if you never watch an ad.
  • When you watch an ad: the SDK records the ad interaction, for example that the video was shown and completed. Coins are then added to your local save.
  • Offline: no ad data is sent while your device has no internet connection. The game itself is fully playable offline.

5. Your choices

  • Ads are optional. You never have to watch an ad to play.
  • Consent: where a consent message applies, you can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads & Privacy → Privacy Options inside the game.
  • Advertising ID: on most Android devices, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • Google ad settings: myadcenter.google.com.
  • Stop all collection by uninstalling the game.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, storing information on your device and personalized advertising rely on your consent, collected through Google's consent message. You can withdraw it at any time as described in section 5. Some processing, such as fraud prevention and serving non-personalized ads, may rely on legitimate interests, as explained in the consent message and in Google's policies.

7. Data retention

  • On-device data is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies; see How Google retains data.
  • Zentrix Arcade does not keep any personal data about you.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because Zentrix Arcade does not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google (see Google's Privacy Policy and My Ad Center). You can also contact us at huydo1805@gmail.com and we will help where we can. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Children

Neon Rush is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you have concerns, contact us and we will help, for example by explaining how to delete the app's data and reset the device's advertising ID.

10. International transfers and security

Google may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under its own safeguards. Communication between the game and Google's services is encrypted (HTTPS). Data saved by the game stays in the app's private storage on your device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game adds features or services. The current version is always available at this address, with its effective date at the top.
